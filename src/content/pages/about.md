I'm an MS CS student at **Columbia University**, focusing on ML systems. I'm also interested in evals.

I'm currently a graduate TA for a **Scaling LLMs: Systems, Optimization, and Emerging Paradigms** seminar class taught by [Professor Kaoutar El Maghraoui](https://research.ibm.com/people/kaoutar-el-maghraoui), where I support students through discussions ranging from inference scaling to agentic AI and tool use. The course also covers hands-on profiling and optimization of custom Triton/CUDA kernels, distributed serving architectures (vLLM, SGLang), and dynamic memory management (KV cache eviction, PagedAttention).

Before Columbia, I studied Computer Science & Statistics at the **University of Michigan**, where I worked in the [SymbioticLab](https://symbioticlab.org/), where I worked on a prototype of [FedScale](https://github.com/SymbioticLab/FedScale) and got hooked on large-scale data systems. 

I write here to make sense of what I'm learning, mostly notes on machine learning and the systems and math behind it.

Outside of work, I enjoy playing the piano, while swimming 🏊, tennis 🎾, and pickleball 🥒 are my sports of choice.
